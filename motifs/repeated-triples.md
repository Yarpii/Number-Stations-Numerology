# Repeated Triples

## Definition

A repeated triple is a number, group, or transmission block repeated three times.

Examples:
- `845 845 845`
- three relay transmissions
- three header repeats
- threefold null markers

## Why this motif matters

Threefold repetition is one of the clearest recurring numeric behaviors in station culture because it can be:
- procedural
- mnemonic
- symbolic
- redundancy-driven

## Operational explanations

- better reception odds
- authentication through repetition
- synchronization
- habit inherited from earlier formats

## Symbolic readings

- triad
- emphasis
- completion
- sealing
- invocation-like insistence

## Strong starter example

V07 is the best initial case in this repo because Priyom documents a three-part relay pattern with 20-minute spacing.

- station
