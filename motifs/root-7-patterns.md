# Root-7 Patterns

## Definition

A root-7 pattern is any numeric set whose digital root resolves to **7**.

Examples:
- 16 → 7
- 34 → 7
- 52 → 7
- 115 → 1 + 1 + 5 = 7

## Why track it?

In symbolic traditions, 7 is often associated with secrecy, hidden knowledge, taboo, inwardness, or sacred structure.  
In radio analysis, that meaning is not assumed — but root-7 clustering can still be studied as a pattern.

## Good use cases

- group counts
- call-up IDs
- counters
- schedule dates
- frequency sums
- month/day combinations

## Risks

Root analysis is easy to overuse.  
Without a baseline comparison, almost any dataset can be made to "look meaningful."

## Safe method

1. pick one data field
2. compute digital roots for the whole sample
3. compare with roots 1–9
4. only then ask whether 7 is unusually dense
