# V07

## Summary

V07 is a highly valuable case study because its format openly foregrounds **threefold repetition**, **numeric headers**, and **zero-heavy null markers**. For this repository, it is one of the clearest examples of how a station can be operationally structured in ways that also invite symbolic reading.

## Documented profile

- **Enigma ID:** V07
- **Operator family:** linked by Priyom to the Russian 7 operator family
- **Language:** Spanish
- **Status:** active in recent monitoring culture
- **Core format feature:** the same transmission is sent three times, 20 minutes apart
- **Null marker:** `000 000`

## Why V07 matters here

V07 exposes several distinct numeric layers at once:

- repeated call-up IDs
- group counts
- body groups
- null markers
- triple transmission cycles
- stepped frequency shifts between repeats

That makes it ideal for motif-based classification.

## Observed numeric functions

### 1. Triple transmission cycle
Priyom states that the sole V07 schedule consists of **3 identical transmissions**, each separated by **20 minutes**.

Operational reading:
- redundancy
- improved chance of reception
- propagation insurance

Symbolic reading:
- triad
- emphasis
- sealing
- repetition as insistence

### 2. Zero-heavy ending
Priyom's Russian 7 operator page identifies **`000 000`** as a characteristic feature for null messages and outro behavior.

Operational reading:
- no-traffic signal
- closure marker
- explicit null state

Symbolic reading:
- void
- absence
- silence encoded as number
- closure through emptiness

### 3. 3-digit ID and group-count logic
The station format includes a 3-digit ID and a group count before the body. That means numbers in V07 are not just content — they also define message structure.

## Pattern significance

V07 is especially good for studying:
- the meaning of repeated triples
- numeric closure formulas
- differences between full and null traffic
- stepped frequency behavior across a relay chain

## Suggested research tasks

- compare full messages with null messages
